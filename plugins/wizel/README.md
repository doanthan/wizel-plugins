# Wizel

Plan, make and review email marketing for your Shopify stores in Wizel, from Claude.

## Use it

Add the plugin, click **Connect** beside Wizel and sign in with your Wizel account. Ask Claude what is waiting on you this week, how last month went for a store, to plan a sale or an event, or to make an email in the brand's own voice. Claude works through the Wizel connector and gives you a Wizel link to approve, schedule or publish.

In Claude Code:

```bash
/plugin install wizel --marketplace doanthan/wizel-plugins
```

## Data

The plugin connects Claude to your Wizel account at www.wizel.ai/api/mcp/mcp. Claude sends Wizel the store you pick and what you ask it to look up or change; Wizel returns your stores' figures, plans and emails. You choose which stores the connection reaches, and whether it can change anything, when you sign in. Revoke it any time in Wizel under Account settings, AI connections.
